The name

Quixotic

kwik-SOT-ik · adjective

Chasing a goal that everyone else calls impossible.

Don Quixote charged at windmills while people called him a fool. We take the name as a promise, not a warning: the dream is just as big, but this time the story ends with it built.

Our first game · Codename Defiant Signal

A security robot wakes on a ship that crashed long ago

Its fellow robots have adapted to the world outside. To its programming they look like corruption, so it hunts them down, until it reaches their leader.
